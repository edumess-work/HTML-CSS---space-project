Our Solar System

OUR COSMIC NEIGHBOURHOOD

Eight Worlds. One Solar System.

Our solar system consists of the Sun, eight planets, dwarf planets, moons, asteroids and comets. Each world has its own characteristics and fascinating features.

Mercury
smallest planet in our solar system and the closest to the Sun. Its surface experiences extreme temperature changes.

Venus
A world covered in thick clouds. Venus is the hottest planet in our solar system because of its powerful greenhouse effect.

Earth

Our home planet. Earth has liquid water on its surface and is the only world currently known to support life.

Mars
called the Red Planet because of its reddish appearance. Scientists study Mars to understand its past and whether it could once have supported microbial life.

Jupiter
largest planet in our solar system. Jupiter is a gas giant known for its enormous storms and many moons.

Saturn

Famous for its spectacular rings, Saturn is a gas giant surrounded by countless particles of ice and rock.

Uranus
ice giant with an unusual rotation, making it appear to spin almost on its side.

Neptune
distant ice giant known for its deep blue appearance and extremely powerful winds.

Stars

LIGHT ACROSS THE COSMOS

The Engines of the Universe.

Stars are enormous spheres of hot gas that produce energy through nuclear fusion in their cores. They create the light that allows us to observe distant parts of the universe. From our Sun to enormous stars far beyond our solar system, each one has a story of its own.
