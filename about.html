------- About Orbit

Curiosity
Has No
Boundaries

ORBIT is a digital space and science magazine created to make the universe easier and more interesting to explore. We believe that learning about space should not be limited to scientists and researchers. Anyone with curiosity and a willingness to ask questions can begin exploring the cosmos.

Our Purpose

Making the Universe Easier to Understand.

Space science can sometimes seem complicated, especially when exploring ideas that are far beyond everyday human experience. ORBIT aims to present astronomy and space-related topics in a clear, accessible and engaging way. From understanding the planets to learning about distant galaxies, our goal is to make scientific knowledge more approachable.
